03 / Engagement models

Start small. Make the outcome explicit.

You don't need to commit to a large contract to see whether we work well together. We can begin with a paid task or a small milestone.

Fixed-scope build

A defined feature, integration, API endpoint, workflow or internal tool with agreed deliverables and acceptance checks.

Paid technical spike

A short, compensated investigation or prototype to validate feasibility, integration risk or an architecture decision before a larger build.

Hourly / ongoing support

Small engineering batches, debugging, test improvements or iterative feature work with a written task list and time budget.

◈Clear payment boundary: I agree scope, rate or fixed fee, acceptance criteria and payment timing before starting. I don't treat unpaid production work as a hiring test. If an assessment is required, its compensation is agreed in advance.
04 / How it works

Four steps. No mystery handoff.

Good projects begin with a useful brief and end with a clear handover.

Send the brief

Describe the problem, current stack, constraints and the outcome you need.

Agree the scope

We define deliverables, exclusions, fee, milestones, acceptance checks and payment timing.

Build and verify

I implement the agreed work, share progress and test the important paths and failure cases.

Demo and hand over

You receive the agreed code, documentation, test evidence and a clear list of remaining limitations.
