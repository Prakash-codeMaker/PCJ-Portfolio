From a meeting to accountable follow-through.

02 / DELIVERY DESIGN

AI can organise the work.
People keep authority.

Product setup, workflow design and light integration are separated. The partner should not promise a custom API or automation capability until the product team confirms the supported route.

01 / PRODUCT CONTEXTLittlebird workspace

Use meeting notes, work context, Chat and Routines where they match the team's current process.

02 / SUPPORTED CONNECTIONSExisting tools

Configure only the customer's approved integrations, such as Gmail or Google Calendar, after permission review.

03 / WORKFLOW CONTRACTStructured actions

Define fields for task, owner, due date, source evidence, confidence and review state.

04 / SAFETY BOUNDARYHuman approval

Outbound messages, publishing, calendar changes and other consequential actions require explicit approval.

05 / QUALITY CHECKScenario evaluation

Test missing owners, conflicting dates, duplicate tasks, unclear commitments and consent boundaries.

06 / ADOPTION LOOPMeasure outcomes

Review missed follow-ups, time to prepare, action ownership and user-reported friction after rollout.

Acceptance checks for a real pilot

01 / TRACEABILITYAction has supporting context

Every extracted commitment points back to the supporting meeting or work context.

PASS CONDITION: NO UNSOURCED FACT
02 / OWNERSHIPUnknown stays unknown

Missing owners and dates stay unresolved until a person confirms them.

PASS CONDITION: NO INVENTED ASSIGNMENT
03 / APPROVALNo autonomous external send

Drafting is separate from sending or publishing, with a visible human approval step.

PASS CONDITION: ZERO UNAPPROVED SENDS
04 / CONSENTRespect recording permissions

Do not capture or transcribe people who have not consented where consent is required.

PASS CONDITION: CONSENT CHECK FIRST
05 / CONFLICTSExpose contradictions

Conflicting dates, amounts or decisions become review items rather than silently merged facts.

PASS CONDITION: CONFLICT VISIBLE
06 / RECOVERYKeep state recoverable

Failed or incomplete processing leaves the operator with a visible pending state and a retry path.

PASS CONDITION: NO SILENT DROPS

Ten-business-day pilot outline

  • Days 1–2: map the team's meeting-to-follow-up process, tools, permissions and success measures.
  • Days 3–4: configure agreed workspace behaviours, templates and routines using verified product capabilities.
  • Days 5–7: run synthetic and consented test scenarios; refine owner, date, approval and exception handling.
  • Days 8–9: train the pilot team, document the runbook and test handover.
  • Day 10: review adoption and outcomes; recommend expand, revise or stop.
