An exploratory essay collection

Beyond the
Chat Box.

What could interacting with intelligence feel like when interaction is no longer confined to a text field? Ten essays on attention, agency, memory, language, embodiment, and human capability.

By Prakash Chand Jain · Conceptual research and design questions · November 2026

A chat window is only one way to interact with intelligence. This collection explores what might emerge when AI can share attention, work across modalities, act with bounded agency, and support human capability—while keeping consent, context, and control visible.

Scope and status. These are exploratory, research-informed essays—not reports of completed user studies or experimentally validated findings. References are starting points for further reading; the essays should be read as hypotheses open to critique.
