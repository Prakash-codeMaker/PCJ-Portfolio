# Beyond the Chat Box

Ten exploratory essays on future directions for human–AI interaction by Prakash Chand Jain.

## Essays
1. [The Chat Box Is a Training Wheel](./01-chat-box-training-wheel.html) — Interaction paradigms
2. [Point to What You Mean](./02-point-to-what-you-mean.html) — Multimodal interaction
3. [Silence Is an Interface State](./03-silence-is-an-interface-state.html) — Voice and turn-taking
4. [The Agent Should Show the Boundary of Its Agency](./04-boundary-of-agency.html) — Agentic interaction
5. [Memory Needs a Front Door and an Exit](./05-memory-front-door-and-exit.html) — Personalisation and control
6. [Code-Switching Is Not a Fallback](./06-code-switching.html) — Multilingual interaction
7. [Ambient AI Must Earn the Right to Interrupt](./07-ambient-ai-interruptions.html) — Attention and ambient computing
8. [When Your Agent Meets My Agent, Who Is Actually Speaking?](./08-agent-to-agent-interaction.html) — Social agents and representation
9. [A Conversation Has More Than One Owner](./09-conversation-ownership.html) — Collaborative AI
10. [The Best AI Interface Makes the Human More Capable](./10-human-capability.html) — Human capability and learning

## Scope
These are research-informed conceptual essays, not reports of completed user studies or experimentally validated findings. Each article develops a design argument, sketches interaction scenarios, identifies potential failure modes, and proposes questions for future research. References are starting points for further reading.
